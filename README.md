## Open. Launch. Enjoy.

1. Open the launcher in your Tesla browser while parked.
2. Tap a Quick App or type a website address.
3. Save the launcher to your browser favorites for next time.

**Free to use. No installation. No launcher account.**

## Made for Your Tesla Screen

| Quick access | Your own setup |
| --- | --- |
| Streaming and social shortcuts | Add your own website links |
| All Apps folder | Reopen recently launched websites |
| Large touch controls | Dark and Light themes |

An internet connection is required. Playback and fullscreen availability depend on your Tesla browser and the service. Streaming subscriptions are separate.

<details>
<summary><strong>Questions, compatibility and privacy</strong></summary>

Use entertainment websites only while parked. Some services may be unavailable in your region or unsupported by the Tesla browser.

Your apps and preferences are saved in the same browser, without device sync. Clearing browser data may remove them.

The launcher has no built-in analytics. Fonts and favicons load from external providers; websites you open have their own privacy policies.

Need help? Open **Help & Connection Guide** in the launcher or [report a problem](https://github.com/BFMIX/Tesla-fullscreen-launcher/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Never share passwords or private links.

</details>
